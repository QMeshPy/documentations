Finance and risk jobs need prices, and a price you cannot check is a weak input. QMesh reads **Chainlink Data Feeds** over
JSON-RPC, validates every round, and records the exact rounds it used in the job's provenance so the inputs can be re-read
later. There are two uses: an ETH/USD feed on Ethereum Sepolia, and price history for **Robinhood tokenized stocks**.

<Iso scene="chainlink" />

## ETH/USD on Ethereum Sepolia

[#98](https://github.com/QMeshPy/distributed-quantum/pull/98) adds optional Chainlink ETH/USD pricing to European call
pricing, risk analysis and portfolio valuation.

- It validates feed freshness, price deviation and block consistency.
- It keeps historical inputs for risk and portfolio calculations.
- It records oracle observations and reproducible inputs with verifiable hashes.
- Provenance files are downloadable with authentication, and the console signs its finance requests.
- Only Ethereum Sepolia ETH/USD is supported. An unsupported portfolio asset is marked as such. **No price is substituted.**

## Robinhood tokenized stocks

Three pull requests ([#106](https://github.com/QMeshPy/distributed-quantum/pull/106),
[#107](https://github.com/QMeshPy/distributed-quantum/pull/107) and
[#108](https://github.com/QMeshPy/distributed-quantum/pull/108)) let you optimize a portfolio of stock tokens from the
console's finance page.

<Callout type="warn" title="One deliberate read-only mainnet exception">
  Robinhood's Stock Tokens and their Chainlink feeds exist on Robinhood Chain **mainnet** only. The registry keeps only the
  testnet. Price history is read from mainnet with `eth_call` alone: no wallet, no signature, no transaction.
</Callout>

### The nine assets

AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, AMZN, META, TSLA, SPY and QQQ. The list is a reviewed allowlist that joins Robinhood's asset
list with Chainlink's feed directory. Each entry was checked on-chain: the token's symbol, decimals and multiplier, and the
feed's description and decimals.

### The data rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Pricing | Token prices. The feeds already include the token multiplier, which is never applied again. |
| Sampling | Each weekday's price is the last valid round at or before **21:00 UTC** (after the US close), if it is at most 36 hours old. |
| Gaps | A weekday on which no selected feed published (a market holiday) is dropped. Nothing is zero-filled. |
| Launch rounds | Each feed's first rounds were published at about 100 million times the price, and are excluded. |
| Range | At least 60 weekdays and at most 366 days, with no future end date. |
| Fail closed | A bad answer, a move above 50% between rounds, feed drift, a feed silent for more than 7 days, or fewer than 60 aligned trading days rejects the history. |

### Use it

```bash
curl http://localhost:8081/api/v1/finance/tokenized-assets
```

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8081/api/v1/finance/tokenized-portfolio \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"symbols": ["AAPL", "MSFT", "SPY"], "start_date": "2026-06-23", "end_date": "2026-10-28"}'
```

The request is validated first: an unsupported or duplicate asset, a bad or future date, a range under 60 weekdays or an
unacceptable `budget` is answered with `422` before any job exists. A valid request queues a normal finance job. When it
completes, `GET /api/v1/finance/{job_id}` adds:

- `market_data`: the source, chain, block, aligned date range, and per-asset token and feed addresses with first and last round ids.
- `allocation_weights`: the optimizer's equal weights over each method's selected assets.
- `disclaimer`: analysis only, not investment advice. Nothing is signed or traded.
- `provenance`: every daily close as a Chainlink observation, so the inputs can be re-read by round id.

Insufficient or invalid history fails the job with a fixed message. At most two history reads run at once per process, because
the public RPC is rate-limited. Set `QB2_ROBINHOOD_MAINNET_RPC_URL` to use your own.

[#115](https://github.com/QMeshPy/distributed-quantum/pull/115) later compacted the provenance (removing duplicated
observations) and made the backend serve provenance downloads byte for byte so hashes still match.

<Callout>
  Stock Tokens are not offered to U.S. persons or in restricted jurisdictions, and QMesh only analyzes them. It never
  holds, trades or recommends them.
</Callout>

## In review

[#123](https://github.com/QMeshPy/distributed-quantum/pull/123) adds a read-only **wallet portfolio** analyzer:
`POST /api/v1/risk/analyze-wallet` prices an address's holdings (or an ENS name) and runs the same VaR and CVaR engine.
A holding that cannot be priced is listed, not dropped. It is open and may change.
